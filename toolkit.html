🔎Personal Data Breach Checker

Have I Been Pwned tells you which known data breaches included your email address.

  1. Open Have I Been Pwned.
  2. Type your email address and press the search button.
  3. Read the list of breaches and which details leaked, such as passwords or phone numbers.
  4. If your email is listed, change that password now and follow the fix in the First-Aid Kit.
  5. Check every email address you use, not just one.

🎣Phishing and Malicious URL Detector

VirusTotal checks a link against dozens of security engines before you open it.

  1. Do not open the suspicious link. Copy it instead: press and hold on a phone, or right-click and choose Copy link address on a computer.
  2. Open VirusTotal, choose the URL tab and paste the link.
  3. Press Enter and wait for the result.
  4. Read how many engines flagged the link. Any warning means you should stay away from it.
  5. You can also look the site up in Google Safe Browsing.

🔑Password Security Audit

Google Password Checkup finds weak, reused or compromised passwords saved in your Google account.

  1. Open Google Password Manager and sign in.
  2. Choose Checkup.
  3. See which passwords are weak, reused or exposed in a breach.
  4. Change the riskiest ones first, starting with email and banking.
  5. Consider a password manager such as Bitwarden to keep a different password for every site.

🎭Social Media Privacy and Impersonation Checker

Review who can see your profile, tags and location, and find where to report a fake account.

  1. Open the Instagram Help Centre, or the Facebook Help Centre if you use Facebook.
  2. In your account settings, make your profile private and check who can tag or mention you.
  3. Hide your activity status and location where you can.
  4. Search your own name and username to see if a fake profile exists.
  5. If you find one, follow the fix in the First-Aid Kit.

📱App Permission and Privacy Auditor

See which apps can reach your camera, microphone, location and photos, and switch off what they do not need.

  1. On Android, open Settings, then Privacy, then Permission manager.
  2. On an iPhone, open Settings, then Privacy and Security. Safety Check can reset access in one go.
  3. Look at Camera, Microphone, Location and Photos first.
  4. Switch off anything an app does not need, or set it to only while you use the app.
  5. Uninstall apps you no longer use.

📍Photo Metadata Privacy Checker

Check what hidden details a photo carries, like GPS location, the time and your device.

  1. Open the metadata viewer.
  2. Upload a photo from your device, or paste the web address of a photo.
  3. Look for GPS location, the date and time, and the camera or phone model.
  4. If you see a location, remove it before you post the photo.
  5. Verexif can strip the metadata for you.
